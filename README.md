# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple Bash script that calculates simple interest based on principal, annual interest rate, and time in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = Principal × Annual Rate × Time

## Usage

Run:

```bash
bash simple-interest.sh
```

Enter the principal amount, annual rate of interest (as a percentage), and time period in years when prompted.

## Example

For a principal of 1000, rate of 5%, and time of 2 years, the simple interest is 100.
